# Art reference prompts

Working title: **Frontline Foundry** (original name, original look; mechanics are the military-tycoon PvP genre).

Shared style line (append to every prompt):
> Stylized low-poly 3D, flat shading, chunky readable silhouettes, muted olive / sand / slate palette with one saturated team accent color, soft daylight, no text, no logos, no real-world brands or insignia.

Drop finished images in `client/assets/reference/` using the file name in each heading.

## 1. `key_art.png` — overall mood
A sunny coastal island battlefield seen from a high three-quarter angle. Several fortified player bases sit around the edge of the island, each a square concrete plot with factory machines, conveyor belts and sandbag walls. The center of the island is an open contested zone with a ruined radio tower, craters and a dirt road network. A small tank, a jeep and a helicopter are mid-action. 16:9.

## 2. `base_progression.png` — tycoon base, 4 stages
A 2x2 grid showing the same square base plot from a top-down three-quarter view at four stages of growth: (1) bare concrete pad with one small ore dropper, a short conveyor and a collector bin; (2) three droppers, a longer conveyor, a fence and a small barracks; (3) walls with a gate, a guard tower, a vehicle pad, a weapons rack; (4) a fully built fortress with a helipad, a tank garage, an anti-air turret and a large factory hall. Ground-level glowing pressure pads mark the next buyable items.

## 3. `vehicles_sheet.png` — vehicle turnaround
Orthographic model sheet, white background, four vehicles in side and three-quarter views: a light four-seat jeep with a roll cage, a medium tank with a rotating turret, a two-rotor transport helicopter, a single-prop ground-attack plane. Simple box-and-cylinder construction suitable for primitive meshes.

## 4. `gear_sheet.png` — equipment props (revised; the first version was refused)
A prop sheet for a cartoon toy-soldier game, white background, side views evenly spaced in one row: a small handheld blaster, a long blaster, a compact blaster, a long-scoped marksman blaster, a wide double-barrel blaster, a shoulder-mounted tube launcher, a round canister, a short utility tool. Chunky plastic toy look like moulded action-figure accessories, olive and dark grey with small orange details, rounded blocky shapes, no realistic detail.

## Style notes taken from received references
- Soldiers: blocky olive camo; team accent on helmet stripe, shoulders, cuffs, knees (orange / teal).
- Bases: grey concrete walls with amber lights, yellow/black hazard trim on machines, glowing amber floor buy pads.
- Vehicles: two-tone olive/khaki camo. Map: sandy dirt roads, pines, grey boulders, blue water.

## 5. `soldiers.png` — character silhouettes
Two blocky low-poly soldier characters standing in a neutral A-pose, front and back views. Same body proportions (slightly oversized head and hands for readability). Team A uses rust-orange accents, team B uses teal accents; otherwise identical olive fatigues, helmet and vest.

## 6. `map_overview.png` — map layout
Top-down orthographic map of an island roughly square in shape. Eight base plots evenly spaced around the coast, each with a short road to a ring road. A central plateau with capture points, a ruined town in one quadrant, a forest in another, an airfield in a third, a quarry in the fourth. Clean game-map illustration style with soft colors, no labels.

## 7. `ui_mockups.png` — interface style
A 2x2 grid of flat game UI mockups in one consistent style (dark slate panels, rounded corners, olive accent, bold sans-serif placeholder lines instead of real text): (1) login screen; (2) room browser list with "create" and "archived" tabs; (3) in-game HUD with health, ammo, cash counter, minimap, kill feed; (4) host admin panel with a player list and action buttons.

## 8. `textures.png` — seamless tiles
A 3x2 grid of seamless tileable textures, flat lighting, 512x512 each: short grass, packed dirt, concrete slab with joints, diamond-plate metal, sand, camouflage netting.

## 9. `buy_icons.png` — buyable item icons
A 6x4 grid of square flat icons on transparent background, consistent outline style: dropper, conveyor, collector, upgrader, wall, gate, guard tower, turret, barracks, jeep, tank, helicopter, plane, helipad, pistol, rifle, sniper, rocket launcher, grenade, armor vest, medkit, cash stack, rank chevron, rebirth star.
